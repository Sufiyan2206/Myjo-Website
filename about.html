About MYJO

Life never halts, so why should learning?

MYJO Smart Schooling keeps children learning and growing, whether classes are online or offline, by bringing school into the home.

Who we are

An innovative approach to learning

Children are, by nature, inquisitive and creative. We believe that when given ownership of their goals, children will use that creativity to reach them.

MYJO does exactly that, with immersive self-learning material in the form of hands-on activities, brain-based projects, gamified learning capsules and more, delivered in a monthly resource kit.

Our vision

Access to quality education for every child, in every home, with a research-driven method that builds a lifelong love for learning.

Children should continue to learn and transform into contributors to the world.

MS Research Foundation

Curated by learning specialists

MYJO is curated by learning specialists at MS Research Foundation (MSRF). Its curriculum is developed by researchers, psychologists and subject matter experts.

  • A blend of online and offline instruction, supported by teachers
  • Tailored to address each student's learning needs and styles
  • Helps students become independent learners
  • Turns parents and guardians into learning guides
  • Works whatever a family's access to school premises or technology
