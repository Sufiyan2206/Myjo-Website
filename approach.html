Our approach

The Heutagogy Framework

Based on heutagogy, which promotes self-determined learning, we identified learning goals and lessons for life for each developmental milestone, and created lesson designs to help each child succeed.

Self-determined learning

What is heutagogy?

Heutagogy puts the learner in charge. Children learn to identify their own learning needs, set goals and reflect on progress, with parents and teachers guiding rather than directing.

At MYJO, this means innovative trackers, creative activities and lesson designs that help children become independent, lifelong learners.

Learning principles

The research behind every lesson

The MYJO framework combines proven learning research into practical lesson designs for home-based schooling.

  • Holistic approach: physical, intellectual, emotional and spiritual
  • Brain-based learning that makes learning natural
  • Stages of development and psychological needs
  • Activities for various learning styles
  • Constructivism: building on prior knowledge
  • Contextual learning with the 5E approach
  • Values integrated into each activity
  • Age-appropriate skills in each activity
  • Realistic and attainable goals
  • Hands-on learning experiences
  • Integration across concepts through themes
  • Active engagement
  • Process-based learning
  • Levelled assessments with higher-order thinking
  • Synergy projects for collaboration and social development
