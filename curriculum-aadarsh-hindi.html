Coursebook, Classes 1–5

Language learnt best as part of a child’s world

Stories, pictures, poetry, conversation and meaningful activities help learners engage with Hindi naturally: observing, listening, understanding, responding and expressing with confidence.

जहाँ हर भाषा-अनुभव बच्चे को अपनी बात कहने का अवसर देता है।

Where every language experience gives a child the chance to say what they think.

Key features and NEP 2020 alignment

  • Picture Story · चित्र कथा

    Picture sequences that turn observation into storytelling.

  • Teaching Points · शिक्षण बिंदु

    Clear learning goals for every lesson.

  • Picture and Talk · चित्र और बातचीत

    Guided conversation from words to sentences.

  • Counting · गिनती

    Hindi numbers, recognised, read and written.

  • Poem / Rhyme · कविता

    Rhythm, sound and imagery to feel the language.

  • Language Learning · भाषा सीखने

    Written and spoken pronunciation skills.

  • Tarbiyah

    Kindness, respect, responsibility and empathy.

  • Activity Time (EL)

    Hindi used beyond the book, in everyday contexts.

  • 21st Century Skills

    Future-ready skills through story, talk and poetry.

  • Cross-Curricular Links

    Connections to surroundings and other subjects.

  • SDG

    People, nature and our shared responsibility.

  • 360° Holistic Development

    Language, thinking, creativity and values together.

Not just reading Hindi: understanding and expressing it

  • Language as experience. Learners observe, listen, understand, respond and express with growing confidence.
  • Pictures and conversation. Picture stories and guided talk move learners from words to sentences.
  • Tarbiyah and character. Relatable situations explore kindness, respect, responsibility and care.
  • Holistic, future-ready growth. Language, thinking, creativity and real-world awareness together.
Workbook, Classes 1–3

Step-by-step practice, purposefully sequenced

Guided practice, interactive activities and age-appropriate progression move learners from recognition and understanding to practice and independent application.

  • Activity-Based Learning · गतिविधि

    Tracing, ticking, matching, colouring, sequencing and sorting: learning by doing.

  • Tracing Letters · पढ़िए और लिखिए

    Multi-line ruling guides the Shirorekha, letter body and descenders for neat, even handwriting.

  • Two-Letter Word Formation · दो अक्षर वाले शब्द

    Read-and-write practice that moves learners from single letters to simple words.

  • Hindi Numbers · गिनती

    Numerals, Hindi digits and number names linked through a read-and-write routine.

Better together. Numbered, dated worksheets build steady progress, from tracing letters to reading and writing words and numbers. लिखो। सीखो। आत्मविश्वास से आगे बढ़ो। (Write. Learn. Move forward with confidence.)
