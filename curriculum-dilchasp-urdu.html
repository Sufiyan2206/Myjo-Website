Coursebook, Classes 1–5

Structured, sequential, steadily confident

A gradual approach strengthens the core pillars of reading, writing and vocabulary, so learners understand the language, apply it accurately in context and express their ideas with clarity.

Four steps to confident Urdu

  1. Step 1Recognise letters
  2. Step 2Read with fluency
  3. Step 3Write with accuracy
  4. Step 4Express with confidence

Key features and NEP 2020 alignment

  • Read and Enjoy

    Poetry on faith, gratitude and nature that builds a love of language.

  • Trace and Write

    Guided stroke formation for correct letter construction and pencil grip.

  • Practice

    Purposeful exercises for letter recognition and word-building.

  • Read Fluently

    Vowel marks and elongations such as Madd (آ) for confident reading.

  • Listen and Understand

    Listening, Speaking, Reading and Writing for fluent communication.

  • Mental Map

    Visual organisation of words and ideas for lasting recall.

  • Tarbiyah

    Character lessons on respect, helping others and hard work.

  • Experiential Learning

    Activity Time: colouring, drawing and hands-on tasks.

  • 21st Century Skills

    Critical thinking, communication and collaboration.

  • Co-Curricular Activities

    Links beyond the textbook, such as exploring cultural landmarks.

  • SDG

    Global themes that build awareness and responsibility.

  • 360° Holistic Development

    Empathy, gratitude, integrity and mutual respect.

Workbook, Classes 1–3

From letters to meaningful words

Guided practice and carefully structured activities in reading, writing and vocabulary help learners move beyond recognising individual letters to forming, reading and writing meaningful words.

  • Letter Sequence & Dictation

    Join the letters in order to complete a picture, alongside dictation practice. Read with understanding.

  • Word Breakdown & Blending

    Split words into letters and blend letters into words to master structure and spelling. Write with greater accuracy.

  • Picture-Based Vocabulary

    Identify familiar objects and connect them with the correct Urdu word. Express with confidence.

Better together. The Workbook extends every Coursebook skill into purposeful practice. Numbered, dated worksheets make progress easy for teachers to follow.

Why Dilchasp Urdu

  • Structured and sequential. A gradual approach that helps learners build sustainable progress.
  • Communication first. Integrated Listening, Speaking, Reading and Writing experiences.
  • Tarbiyah and character. Respect for parents and teachers, helping others and working hard.
  • Holistic, future-ready growth. Empathy, gratitude, integrity and 21st century skills.

Learning that develops knowledge, skills, character and confidence together.
