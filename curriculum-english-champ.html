Coursebook, Classes 1–5

More than a subject: a gateway to expression

Rooted in the vision of NEP 2020, English Champ moves beyond conventional language instruction. Every story, poem, activity and discussion is designed to nurture curiosity, creativity, empathy and critical thinking, and to connect the classroom with the real world.

Sixteen features, one complete language programme

  • Scope & Sequence

    A mapped, progressive pathway of competencies.

  • Story Insights

    Reflection and discussion that link stories to life.

  • Summary

    Identify key ideas and synthesise information.

  • Vocabulary & Grammar

    Accuracy built through language in context.

  • Mind Map

    Visual connections for deeper, lasting recall.

  • Integrated LSRW

    Listening, Speaking, Reading and Writing together.

  • Speaking Corner

    Role-plays, discussions and presentations.

  • Creative Writing

    Guided writing for original ideas and emotions.

  • Word Power

    Discover, understand and apply new words.

  • Reading Comprehension

    Interpret, analyse, evaluate and respond.

  • Cross-Curricular Links

    English connected to Science, Maths, Social Studies and Art.

  • Tarbiyah

    Empathy, gratitude, integrity and respect.

  • Experiential Learning

    Project work that takes learning beyond the page.

  • SDGs

    Global awareness and responsible citizenship.

  • 21st Century Skills

    Critical thinking, creativity and collaboration.

  • 360° Holistic Development

    Intellectual, emotional, social and ethical growth.

Workbook, Classes 1–3

Skill practice that makes language stick

Structured, engaging exercises in reading, writing, grammar and vocabulary help learners apply what they have learnt, and use English accurately, confidently and effectively.

  • Rhyming Words

    Colourful, picture-based activities build phonics awareness, vocabulary and spelling.

  • Story Recall & Critical Thinking

    Story sequencing paired with reflection questions that encourage empathy.

  • Speaking, Reading & Pre-Sentence Writing

    Everyday conversations and guided, picture-based sentence construction.

  • Assessment Worksheets

    Sentence completion and rhyming tasks to check vocabulary, comprehension and spelling.

Better together. Every worksheet is mapped to its Coursebook chapter. Worksheet 4, for example, practises Chapter 2, “Finding Home with Tara”, so practice always follows learning.
