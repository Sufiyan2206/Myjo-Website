Five ways of thinking, built into every worksheet

  1. ExploreAnalytical thinking

    Observe, deconstruct and examine the world systematically.

  2. CompareAnalogical thinking

    Compare man-made and God-made things to make abstract ideas relatable.

  3. QuestionCritical thinking

    Question assumptions and evaluate evidence through inquiry.

  4. ConnectMeditative thinking

    Reflect on the order, balance and harmony of natural systems.

  5. AppreciateMoral thinking

    Turn knowledge into ethical action and empathy.

Workbook, EVS Classes 1–2 · Science Star Class 3

Learning connected to the child’s own surroundings

Activity-based exercises and real-life connections help learners observe, identify and relate concepts to their surroundings, and apply their knowledge in everyday life.
