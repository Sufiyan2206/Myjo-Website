Coursebook, Classes 1–5

Concrete. Pictorial. Abstract.

A tactile-to-symbolic progression moves learners from handling physical manipulatives to decoding abstract symbols, replacing rote memorisation with deep conceptual understanding.

How every concept is taught

  1. ConcreteHandle it

    Physical manipulatives and activities

  2. PictorialSee it

    Visual models and illustrations

  3. AbstractSolve it

    Symbols, operations and reasoning

Key features and NEP 2020 alignment

  • Core Numerical Foundations

    Visual models break operations into age-appropriate steps.

  • Remember

    Visual anchors for key rules and formulas.

  • Common Mistakes

    Wrong and right methods compared, step by step.

  • Fun with Maths

    Puzzles, riddles and logical play.

  • Early Maths

    Counting and subitising toward base-10 numbers.

  • Mind Map

    Visual maps of chapters and multi-step problems.

  • SDG

    Real-world data on the environment and sustainability.

  • 21st Century Skills

    Mental maths, reasoning and problem-solving.

  • Tarbiyah

    Value-driven word problems on honesty and fairness.

  • Activity Time (EL)

    Hands-on experiments with real materials.

  • Cross-Curricular Links

    Maths connected to EVS, social science and daily life.

  • Holistic Development

    Maths through social scenarios such as sharing.

Workbook, Classes 1–3

Practice that builds accuracy and confidence

Age- and grade-appropriate exercises strengthen skills through step-by-step practice and problem-solving, ensuring mastery of concepts through continuous practice.

  • Pre-Maths Concepts

    Compare and classify everyday objects by size and height: big and small, tall and short.

  • Spatial Concepts and Positions

    Top and bottom, inside and outside: positional vocabulary for early reasoning.

  • Place Value and Number Sequences

    Tens and ones on the abacus and missing numbers on number lines build number sense.

  • Critical Thinking and Number Puzzles

    A board-game path of numerical clues develops logical reasoning.

Better together. Workbook chapters follow the Coursebook, from Pre-Maths Concepts onward. Learn the concept in the Coursebook, then master it through practice.
