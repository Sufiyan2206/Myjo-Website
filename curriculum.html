Shared foundations

What every MYJO series has in common

NEP 2020 and NCF 2023

Every series is aligned with the National Education Policy and the National Curriculum Framework.

Tarbiyah

Values such as empathy, gratitude, honesty and respect are built into lessons in every subject.

Mind Maps

Visual summaries help children connect ideas and remember them, from Class 1 to Class 5.

Experiential learning

Activities, projects and real materials turn knowing into doing.

21st century skills

Critical thinking, creativity, communication and collaboration in every chapter.

SDGs and 360° development

Global awareness, sustainability and intellectual, emotional and social growth.

In every class

Learning children can touch, build and explore

Teaching Manuals

Lesson support for every core subject, so teachers can plan and deliver each chapter with confidence.

Student activity material

Craft and sensory materials for each learner: quilling paper, clay, paints, beads, sequins, envelopes and more.

Teaching-learning material

Models, kits and aids for the classroom: skeleton and brain models, geo boards, abacus, fraction strips and more.

Mind Maps, Classes 1 to 5

One central idea, branching into everything that matters. Introduced by Tony Buzan in the 1970s, mind mapping helps learners summarise, take notes and recall information more easily than traditional notes.
