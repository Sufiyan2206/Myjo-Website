Our mission

Life never halts, so why should learning?

Children are naturally curious and creative. When they own their goals, they use that creativity to reach them. MYJO gives them that ownership through hands-on activities, brain-based projects and gamified learning capsules in a monthly resource kit.

About MYJO

Our vision

Access to quality education for every child, in every home, with a research-driven method that builds a lifelong love for learning.

Curated by learning specialists at MS Research Foundation (MSRF), Hyderabad.

The MYJO kit

An all-in-one learning package

Three parts, each designed for a different role in your child's learning.

Fun learning material

Clay, colours, sticks and stationery, organised so children can create, explore and stay focused on each activity.

  • Material that supports creativity and encourages learning
  • Stationery that caters to multiple learning styles
  • QR codes and links to curated online content

Kits are developed by researchers and top educationists, based on the psychological needs of children today.

Student workbook

Self-paced activities that help children set goals, track progress and build independence.

  • Activities based on heutagogical principles of self-paced learning
  • Resources mapped to the child's age and the prescribed curriculum
  • A research-based framework for holistic growth and need-based support

Parent handbook

Clear guidance so parents can support learning at home with confidence.

  • Weekly schedule and expected learning outcomes
  • Parenting calendar with activities for parent-child bonding and grooming
  • Recommendations to guide learning as a mentor and create a good environment at home

Parents also attend orientation webinars to prepare for new themes and challenges.
