Programs

Programs designed for every stage of early learning

Two programs shaped around each child's age, needs, learning style and stage of development, delivered through monthly thematic kits.

Pre-primary

Theme-based learning

Instead of watertight compartments, subjects are taught in sync with transversal competencies, giving children a transdisciplinary learning experience. This helps each child experience the world as a whole.

Activities and experiences under each theme support holistic development. They are chosen to suit the age, needs, learning styles, developmental stage, abilities and interests of every child, so young children achieve quality education through joyful experiences.

Enquire about pre-primary

What children experience

  • Hands-on activities
  • Imagination through arts
  • Cognitive enrichment
  • Language and literacy
  • Rhymes and stories
  • Physical development
  • Skills and values
Primary

An adaptive curriculum

Tailored to address each student's learning needs and styles. Following heutagogy, it makes learners inter-dependent, helping them identify their learning needs and set goals with innovative trackers and creative activities.

Parents are true partners: they become learning guides, supported by well-trained, equipped teachers, in a hybrid system that keeps the joy of learning alive.

Built on these concepts

  • Self-determined learning
  • Constructivism
  • Competency-mapped activities
  • Value-driven learning

  • Ebbinghaus theory of learning and forgetting
